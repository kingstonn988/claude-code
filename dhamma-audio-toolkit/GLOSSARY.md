## Consistency rules

- Keep diacritics throughout, or drop them throughout — do not mix.
- `kilesas` plural, not `kilesa` / `kilesā`, unless he clearly says the singular.
- `citta`, never `the citta mind` — he uses the bare term.
- He says `Than Ajahn` (not `Tan Ajahn`) when referring to his teacher.
