## Type
Playfair Display Black. Gold gradient fill, bright at the top of the letter,
deep amber at the foot. Thin black outline. Size is not fixed — the type grows
to fill the width, so short lines come out big.

**Emphasis**: words marked in the script are set in CAPITALS and painted a
shade brighter. Chosen by hand per verse — it is a judgement about meaning.

**Capitals**: every beat starts with a capital. The recap at the end uses the
book's own sentence case.

## How the words arrive
| Words | Style |
|---|---|
| up to 14 | lines build and stay, then a light sweeps across the finished verse |
| 15 and over | one beat at a time, large; then the whole reflection assembles and holds |

## Timing — the verse decides, at pace 1.45
| | |
|---|---|
| Lead-in | 0.35 s — the first words land almost at once |
| Beat dwell | (0.42 + 0.34 per word) x 1.45, never under 1.9 s |
| Line step | (0.30 + 0.28 per word) x 1.45 |
| Final hold | (1.2 + 0.30 per word) x 1.45, capped at 8 s |

## Background
Real photographs, 24 in the library, **rotated** — no section mapping.
Slow push in of 12% across the clip. Warm grade: +10% red, −14% blue,
saturation 0.88, brightness −0.10.

## Music
15 tracks, all normalised to −23 LUFS so no clip is louder than another.
Looped if short, 1.5 s fade in and out, laid on after the picture is finished.
