## What I would do differently

I checked openings for chitchat because that is where you first caught it,
and did not think to check endings until you found three minutes of
breakfast arrangements at the close of a five-minute video. The lesson was
that a splitter cutting on silence has no idea where meaning starts or
stops — only reading does.

The same applies to the automatic detectors. Every one I tried flagged
teaching as chitchat: the tape recorder that is his example of how the mind
makes symbols, the tiger on the cliff, the businessman. Reading was the
only thing that worked.
