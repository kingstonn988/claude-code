# The three collections, and which of your files belong to each

Your three categories are the three published parts of *The Dhamma of Ajaan
Paññāvaḍḍho*:

| Your name for it | Published title | Talks in the full set |
|---|---|---|
| Advice to lay people | **Part 1 — The Basics of Buddhist Practice** | 26 |
| Samādhi and Jhāna | **Part 2 — Samādhi and Wisdom** | 20 |
| For monks | **Part 3 — Teachings for the Monks** | 21 |

## How this was worked out

Your numbered files contain **two** overlapping series — there are two 02s,
two 03s, two 04s, and so on. That is the fingerprint of two separate
collections. The unnumbered files are the third.

Since each number appears only once within a collection, confirming one
title fixes its twin. Published contents confirmed these directly:

- **Monks:** The Tornado of Self, Freedom Through Discipline, The Delusion
  of Self, Mental Imagery, The Kilesas are the Enemy, Developing the Path
  Factors, The Way to True Freedom, Knowing One's Character
- **Basics:** Introduction to the Sangha, The Five Precepts, Personal
  Impressions, The Nature of Feeling, Buddho

Everything else follows from the collisions.
